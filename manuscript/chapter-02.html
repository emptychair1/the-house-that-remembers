At 8:23 that evening, I wrote an email.

To my parents. To Averie.

I called it:

TODAY'S JOURNEY INSIDE MYSELF

I told them I had recognized a pattern.

Immediate pleasure or relief kept overriding what I actually wanted for my life in the long term.

I told them it happened with sex, drugs, money, relationships, and sometimes my own safety.

I told them what I valued.

I told them I was sad about the consequences. I told them I felt shame and self-hatred about damage I knew had come from choices I had made.

Then I wrote something considerably less dramatic than everything that had led to it.

I wanted to create a pause.

A pause between the impulse and the action.

Enough room to ask whether the thing screaming now was consistent with the life I wanted later.

I did not ask them to fix me. I asked them to understand what I was working on and, if they were willing, help me build enough stability to work on it.

I told them I wanted to stop hiding things because I was ashamed. I told them I wanted to rebuild trust.

With myself.
