The trailer sat on remote land in rural Georgia, surrounded by enough darkness that at night the windows stopped behaving like windows.

The trailer itself was beautiful. Custom carpentry. Tin work. Somebody had made the small space with care, fitted things together deliberately, imposed order on twenty-five feet of enclosure.
