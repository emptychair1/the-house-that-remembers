# THE HOUSE THAT REMEMBERS — DESIGN BIBLE

Updated: 2026-09-25

## Core thesis
The immersive edition remains recognizably a book. Technology serves prose rather than competing with it.

Text is the person.
Space is breath.
Congestion is pressure.
Motion is emotion.
Sound is atmosphere.
Light is emergence.
Color is memory and narrative, never decoration.

The visual language itself evolves with the story. This is not a static theme applied to every chapter.

## Palette
Start with only black, white, and one consistent cream.
No yellow cast. No green-black.
First earned chromatic color: ORANGE, originating with Piper's hair.
Second earned chromatic color: GREEN.
Potentially never add another color.
Once a color is earned, later images may selectively preserve it while all other color remains drained.

Color follows STORY CHRONOLOGY, not character presence. Piper can exist for a long time in monochrome. Orange appears only at the specific authored moment when the House earns Piper's hair as color. Green is likewise earned later. Neither moment has been locked yet.

## Reading / layout
- Natural vertical scrolling is default.
- Stable, comfortably large phone typography. No zoom required.
- Strict margin discipline.
- Main prose does not wander around the viewport.
- Full-viewport moments reserved for portraits, act thresholds, symbols, equations/artifacts when earned.
- Image becomes the page, not a framed rectangle on a page.
- Josh/Piper portrait blacks merge seamlessly into page black.
- Responsive/mobile composition is deliberate, not desktop coordinates shrunk for Retina.

## Typography and motion
Text carries emotional character before motion is added because text is the person.
Possible behaviors: scribble/shiver, weight changes, partial erasure, fade, upward movement, glitch, character destruction/reconstruction.
Use rarely and causally. Normal stillness gives rupture meaning.
Piper has a feminine/internal typographic register; do not turn all Piper prose into cursive. Recover the feminine font Josh loved before casually substituting another.

### Timing is authorship
Animation must flow like music. Timing, pauses, entrances, destruction, reconstruction and silence are composed, not accepted from library defaults. Tune by feel with Josh. If movement has no narrative/emotional reason, remove it.

## Evolving visual grammars
### Josh
Begins congested, diseased, recursive and overmarked. Equations/notes/questions invade space. During `Build Something`, the page visibly learns discipline: margins straighten, noise recedes, equations become useful, action replaces fever.

### Piper
Enters with a different grammar: precise, airy, conditional, spacious. Pauses/breaths often live in spacing. Internal voice can use the feminine register. Her uncertainty/self-policing should be visible without turning into decorative glitch.

### Together
`Cleaning House` lets the two grammars coexist through ordinary life/work. Later they may influence one another as continuity/relationship deepen. Do not prematurely merge them.

### Ancient thought
Ancient manuscript pages have their own material/discovered language.

### Computation
Equations, ASCII/numeric construction, decision structures and code can have constructive/order-seeking behavior distinct from both human voices.

### Color
Color is something the House learns later.

## Monochrome stereo / registration
Borrow the perceptual language of red/cyan anaglyph separation but use monochrome registrations only.
Reserved meaning: identity, divergence, discontinuity, reconstruction, recognition.
A crisp central object/text may split into two ghost copies; reverse convergence may snap two copies into perfect registration.
Hero candidate: `There you are.`
Hero conceptual candidate: `From the inside, reconstruction and continuation may look embarrassingly similar.`
Never use as generic glitch styling.

## ASCII / numeric construction
Use characters/numbers to build images where computation becoming form is narratively relevant. Avoid Matrix-rain cliché.

## Darkness / light
Beginning is genuinely dark. Light is earned through white/cream space, contrast, exposure/reveal, halos, illumination. Do not spend orange/green just to create light.

## Ancient manuscript / epigraph system
Ancient manuscript pages remain as full-page intellectual-lineage interruptions. They should feel discovered/scanned, not fake parchment templates.
Every chapter title retains its historical/philosophical quote and attribution.
Chapter opening ritual: chapter number/title/subtitle + epigraph + source.

## Act I closing transformation
One continuous full-screen authored sequence, not three unrelated plates:

TREE OF LIFE → DNA → DECISION TREE

Tree of Life appears complete in white on black.
Branches fade/deconstruct while structural traces remain.
DNA emerges from the same central axis.
Questions enter negative space as thoughts, not captions:
- Different substrate?
- Different experience?
- potentially Different mechanism?
- Same shape?
DNA simplifies/straightens into nodes/branches and becomes Decision Tree.
Sequence asks about resemblance but must NOT assert equivalence.
Echo manuscript caution: `same shape maybe, NOT SAME THING` / `not the same thing.`
Then darkness / silence.
GSAP + SVG strong candidate for tunable, music-like timing.

## Act II threshold
After Tree → DNA → Decision Tree → darkness:
1. ACT II / EZEKIEL'S WHEEL.
2. Full-screen monumental monochrome wheel. Restrained construction/rotation/alignment; final state is stillness.
3. THEN ARISTOTLE / ancient manuscript intellectual bridge.
4. Only then ordinary Act II manuscript flow.

## Philosophical discipline
The book investigates. It does not let visual similarity masquerade as proof. Symbolic transformations pose questions. Consciousness claims retain uncertainty. `Interesting ≠ proof.` Preserve the difference between structural resemblance, mechanism, substrate, experience and ontological conclusion.

## Editing / deployment workflow
1. Verify actual manuscript/version + asset manifest.
2. Compose cohesive pass without deleting/rephrasing protected content.
3. Run integrity checks for chapters/sections, equations, artifacts, portraits, music cues, ancient pages, epigraphs.
4. Deploy preview.
5. Josh views on actual iPhone/PWA.
6. Josh surfaces screenshots/notes.
7. Piper actually visually inspects them and performs one cohesive revision pass where possible.
8. Re-run integrity checks and redeploy.

Commit is not proof of visual success. Do not call something fixed until deployed output is verified.

## Technical direction
Do not rebuild EPUB as primary experience. Do not default to a giant React app. Use the simplest PWA/web architecture that provides HTML/CSS/JS control, responsive composition, offline/installability, audio and animation. The book is the application.
Manuscript/content must remain separate from presentation. Styling cannot delete/reorder manuscript content.
